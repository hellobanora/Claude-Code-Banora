## Wednesday 14 October: Practical tip / movement

- **Topic:** A five-minute morning mobility routine (topic bank no. 15)
- **Single point:** Five minutes of gentle movement before the day starts is an easy habit to keep, and you need no equipment or floor space beyond a bedroom.
- **Format:** Short video script (about 45 to 60 seconds; four or five simple movements shown at a comfortable pace, with on-screen text naming each one).
- **Call to action:** Try it tomorrow morning and tell us which movement felt best.
- **Compliance notes for the writer:**
  - Frame as general movement, not treatment or rehabilitation. No claim that the routine relieves, fixes or prevents anything (rule 2).
  - Include a plain line: move within a comfortable range, stop if anything hurts, and see a health professional if you have an injury or ongoing pain.
  - Movements to be chosen or approved by James or Paul before filming.
