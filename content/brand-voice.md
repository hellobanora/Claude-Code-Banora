## Voice

Warm, knowledgeable, direct. The patient is the hero; we are the guide. Sound like a knowledgeable friend who happens to be a healthcare professional: simple without being condescending, reassuring without making promises.

- No superlatives without evidence ("the best", "world-class")
- No jargon without explanation
- Active voice
- Contractions, varied sentence length, specific details over generic ones
- Never hard-sell; the call to action should feel like a natural next step
